### How to obtain a commercial license

1. Email **[partnership contact — set `PARTNERSHIP_CONTACT_EMAIL` in your deployment]** or use the partnership form on the InterMUN marketing site.
2. Describe your organization, intended use, scale (delegates, committees, events per year), and timeline.
3. Intermun will respond with licensing terms, which may include a custom development agreement, support plan, or trademark guidelines.

Until a commercial license is granted in writing, **commercial use is not permitted**, even if the underlying code is available under Apache 2.0 for non-commercial contexts.
